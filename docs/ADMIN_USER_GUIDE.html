League Admin User Guide & Operating Manual

📌 Executive Summary: Welcome to Puttermore, the digital command center for the Mobtown Brewing Co. Wednesday Night Social Putting League (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM EST). This official operating manual provides step-by-step instructions for League Commissioners, Admins, and Desk Staff on managing rosters, running Wednesday match nights, recording scores on-the-fly, and rolling over future seasons.

🔑 1. Logging In & Admin Roles

To access admin controls, navigate to puttermore.netlify.app and click 🔑 Login in the top navigation bar.

👥 2. Pre-Season Setup (Teams & Rosters)

In Mobtown Fall 2026, games are played in an ad-hoc format—meaning you do not need to build a rigid pre-scheduled matrix before the season starts. Your main pre-season job is registering teams and player rosters.

✨ Creating Teams
  1. Navigate to Admin Console → 👥 Rosters.
  2. Under Create New Team, enter the Team Name (e.g. Dundalk Putters, Mobtown Misfits).
  3. Pick a custom team accent color and click Create Team.
👤 Registering Players & Assigning Captains
  1. Under Team Rosters, select the team from the team list.
  2. Click + Add Player, enter the player's full name, pick an avatar accent color, and tap Save Player.
  3. Assign Captain: Click the 🧢 Captain badge next to a player's name to designate them as Team Captain.
  4. Remove Player: Click the ❌ Remove icon if a player drops out of the roster.

⚡ 3. Wednesday Match Night Operations

On Wednesday match nights (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM at Mobtown), teams walk up to the 6-cup turf board and challenge each other on-the-fly.

⚡ Recording a Match Score (3-Step Quick Console)

When two teams finish playing a Best-of-3 series:

  1. Open Admin Console → ⚡ Score Console.
  2. Step 1 (Pick 2 Teams): Select Home Team (Team 1) and Away Team (Team 2).
  3. Step 2 (Enter Scores): Enter cups sunk for Game 1, Game 2, and optional Game 3 (leave Game 3 blank if a team won 2-0).
  4. Step 3 (Publish): Click 🏆 Complete & Update Standings.

Standings Points Calculation Rules:

  • 2 Points Series Winner (2-0 or 2-1): Earns 2 points in league standings.
  • 1 Point Game 3 Loser (1-2): Earns 1 point (Game-3 / Overtime bonus point).
  • 0 Points Sweep Loser (0-2): Earns 0 points.
✏️ Recalling or Editing a Match Score

If an incorrect score was submitted by mistake, open Admin Console → 📅 Matches tab. Click ✏️ Edit Score to modify game numbers, or 🗑️ Delete / Recall to remove the match and recalculate standings automatically.

⚙️ 4. Season Rollover & Future Seasons

Puttermore natively supports ongoing future seasons (e.g., Fall 2026, Winter 2026, Spring 2027).

🏆 Launching a New Season
  1. Open Admin Console → ⚙️ Settings → Season Rollover Console.
  2. Under Launch New Season, enter Season Name (e.g. Winter 2026 Mobtown Season), Duration (e.g. 6 Weeks), and Start Date.
  3. Click 🏆 Launch New Season. The app switches to the new active season with 0 matches recorded while preserving team rosters.

❓ 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What if a team forfeits on match night?

A: Go to Admin Console → ⚡ Score Console, select the two teams, enter 6-0, 6-0 for the team that attended, and click Publish.

Q: How does the countdown banner on the home page work?

A: The countdown clock automatically ticks down to Wednesday, September 2nd, 2026 at 6:00 PM EST. When opening time arrives, it automatically transitions from ⏳ COUNTDOWN TO OPENING NIGHT to 🟢 SEASON IN PROGRESS.

Q: How can new admins practice without messing up live data?

A: Always log in under 🧪 Demo Tester (Sandbox Admin) mode. Sandbox mode suppresses database write calls and keeps your test scores strictly in your local browser session memory.
